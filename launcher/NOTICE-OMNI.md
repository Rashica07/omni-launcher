# OMNILauncher: attribution

OMNILauncher is derived from the Horizon launcher (https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon, `launcher/`),
which is released under the MIT licence. The original licence and copyright notice are kept in
`../LICENSE` and must stay with every copy of this code.

Changed for OMNI: the name and brand, the OMNI mark, the OMNI theme and animated backdrop, the links,
the partner slot, and a development-only offline mode. Horizon's game builds are still downloaded
from the Horizon project.

The third-party notices in `../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` still apply.
